Suggested Results Text (Smart-seq3 vs NEBNext-thallus)

To contextualize library performance, we compared gene detectability between low-input Smart-seq3 libraries (non-thallus lifecycle stages) and NEBNext libraries (thallus samples). Across TPM thresholds, Smart-seq3 libraries showed comparable-to-higher detectability (mean detected genes: TPM>0.1, 15,080 vs 14,057; TPM>1, 12,410 vs 11,173; TPM>3, 10,569 vs 9,824; Smart-seq3 vs NEBNext-thallus, respectively). These data indicate that the Smart-seq3 strategy provides robust transcriptome sensitivity while enabling profiling of low-abundance, stage-specific tissues that are difficult to isolate at high input.

Because the compared sample sets represent different biological material (non-thallus vs thallus), this analysis is interpreted as a practical benchmarking comparison rather than a strictly controlled kit-only effect estimate.
